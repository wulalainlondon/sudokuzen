## Long Description（Steam BBCode）

```bbcode
[h1]The Grid Is Not What You Think[/h1]

Most puzzle games end where Sudoku Zenith begins.

This is sudoku stripped of its training wheels and rebuilt as a deep, living system — one that rewards intuition, punishes complacency, and rewards the player willing to go further than any number puzzle has asked them to go before.

[h2]Nine Tiers. One Infinite Descent.[/h2]

Nine difficulty tiers — from [b]Novice[/b] to [b]Zen[/b], [b]Void[/b], [b]Ego-less[/b], [b]Origin[/b], [b]Nirvana[/b], [b]Empty Mirror[/b], [b]Star Tide[/b], and finally [b]Abyssal Chain[/b] — each represent a genuine leap in cognitive demand. The names are not decorative. They describe what happens to a solver who earns them.

[h2]41 Techniques. A Complete School of Logic.[/h2]

Sudoku Zenith contains a fully integrated technique library covering [b]41 solving methods[/b], progressing from foundational patterns like Naked Singles and Hidden Pairs all the way to Advanced Inference Chains (AIC).

Each technique is taught, not just listed. Interactive tutorials walk you through the reasoning. Practice Mode lets you isolate and drill any technique by category until the pattern becomes instinct.

[h2]Wild Mode — Logic as Combat[/h2]

In [b]Wild Adventure Mode[/b], every one of the 41 solving techniques corresponds to a distinct enemy — a creature that can only be defeated by recognizing and executing that specific pattern. Solving a Hidden Triple doesn't just clear cells. It destroys what is standing in your way.

Progress unlocks a skill tree, new equipment, and mentor characters whose stories unfold through the solving itself. Wild Mode is not a gimmick bolted onto a puzzle game. It is the argument that sudoku, at sufficient depth, has always been a form of combat.

[h2]Duo Mode — Real-Time Competitive Solving[/h2]

Challenge another player in [b]Duo Mode[/b] — a live head-to-head solving format where speed, accuracy, and technique selection all matter.

[h2]Key Features[/h2]

[*] [b]Nine Transcendent Difficulty Tiers[/b] — From first principles to the edge of human solving ability

[*] [b]Wild Adventure Mode[/b] — Each technique is an enemy. Defeat them through logic alone
[*] [b]Duo Mode Real-Time Battles[/b] — Head-to-head competitive solving
[*] [b]Global Leaderboards[/b] — Where do you rank among those who reached the Void?
[*] [b]Full Offline Support[/b] — Play anywhere, anytime
[*] [b]Dark Cosmic Aesthetic[/b] — Built for long, meditative sessions
[/list]

[b]The grid goes deeper than you think. Begin the descent.[/b]
```
